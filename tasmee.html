Tatbiiq Xifdinta

تَسْمِيع

Isku celceli Qur'aanka: adiga iyo Sheikhaaga aad dooratay ayaad si isdaba joog ah aayado u akhrin doontaan. Haddii aad khaldo, wuu kuu saxi doonaa cod ahaan.

Browser-kaaga ma taageerayo aqoonsiga codka (Speech Recognition), marka qeybta dhagaysiga si toos ah looguma heli karo. Isku day Chrome ama Edge si aad u isticmaasho dhammaan features-ka. Waxaad wali dhageysan kartaa Sheikhka oo aad akhriso adigoo aan la eegin.
